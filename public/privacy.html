3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases of GDPR Article 6:

  • Legitimate Interests: To communicate with you and respond to your direct queries (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Performance of a Contract / Pre-contractual Measures: To arrange and manage appointments and provide physical therapy services (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

4. Cookies and Web Analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics), or any third-party tracking services. No non-essential cookies are set, and your browsing behavior is not tracked or shared. Because we do not use cookies or analytics, no cookie consent banner is displayed.

5. Data Retention Period

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. General contact inquiries are deleted once the query has been resolved, unless further communication is required. Client records related to massage treatments are stored in compliance with applicable laws in Finland.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject located in the EU/Finland, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request that inaccurate or incomplete data be corrected.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can request the deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request that we restrict how we use your data in specific scenarios.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request the transfer of your data to another organization.
  • Right to Object: You can object to the processing of your data based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at algaymanhandball@gmail.com.

If you believe that your data has been processed in violation of data protection regulations, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutettu): tietosuoja.fi.
